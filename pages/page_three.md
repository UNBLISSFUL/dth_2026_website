## Penpot - A project for my major

### Project introduction

An open source project that would be helpful in my major is called Penpot. It’s an open source design platform aiming to bring designers and developers together to a higher level of collaboration. Penpot is a small/medium team based in Europe (Penpot, n.d.) and is the product of a Spanish based company called Kaleidos (Kaleidos, n.d.).

### How penpot can help my major

I study Digital Design as my major which often entails creating some kind of design product. When we do practical exercises we primarily focus on UX design or UI design. At times we do projects where we need to do some minor coding. For these projects Penpot in particular would be great for making wireframes or higher fidelity prototypes that work closer to a developer workflow while maintaining the essence of a UI design process. These types of projects, where we do both designing and coding, are currently the main draw to use Penpot over proprietary software like Figma.

### The five checks

#### License

Penpot uses the Mozilla Public License Version 2.0 (GitHub - Penpot/Penpot, n.d.) 
Frankly, it’s quite hard to understand what exactly licenses allow and don’t when you have no understanding of legal jargon. From what I can understand the license falls in the “weak” copy-left category. It seems to implement the license on a file-by-file basis (Mozilla Public License, Version 2.0, n.d).

#### Last commit, contributors, issues answered

Last commit was on November 10, 2026 as of November 11. The Penpot repo has 327 contributors. It seems issues are answered, with 3052 issues closed and 836 open. Most of the closed issues seem to be moved to a “Done” status in the main branch (Issues · Penpot/Penpot, n.d.).

#### Funding

Penpot has the company Kaleidos behind them, while also securing $12 million in funding led by Decibel (carolina.portugal, 2023). As well as support from other open source Venture Capitals (Penpot, n.d.).

#### My verdict

I currently use Figma for most of my UI design and could likely leave it again. However, when I’m no longer a student, I will eventually be switching to the free plan of figma and no longer on the EDU plan. Here it would be much less limiting to switch to Penpot.
